Team

  • Bernardo M. Almeida is a Partner at Cloud Hill Capital, where he is responsible for limited partner relations and capital formation, building long-term partnerships with the sovereign, institutional and strategic investors who invest alongside the firm. Prior to Cloud Hill, he founded Avalanche Capital, a placement agency for emerging fund managers, where he advised more than 100 clients and generated over $1 billion in capital introductions. Earlier in his career, he served in Investor Relations at Vanguard Asset Management.

  • Gabriel Korish is a Partner at Cloud Hill Capital, where he leads deal origination and portfolio relations. He is responsible for sourcing growth-stage investment opportunities across the firm’s focus areas and for supporting portfolio companies after investment, connecting them with the partners, capital and resources that accelerate their growth. Gabriel was educated at Harvard Business School.

  • Dale Vine is a Venture Partner at Cloud Hill Capital, where he drives the firm’s strategic relationships and partnerships. He is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of DVC Capital, a single family office, and a General Partner at ArcstoneLC, an AI infrastructure platform investing in European data center capacity. An active investor across artificial intelligence, fintech and real estate, Dale brings an extensive network of founders, family offices and institutional investors across the Gulf, Europe and the United States.

  • John Bivins is a Partner at Cloud Hill Capital, where he focuses on sourcing and investing in high-growth technology companies. Previously, he served as Head of North America at Crewstone International, a Kuala Lumpur–based private equity firm managing sovereign and institutional capital. Throughout his career, he has facilitated more than $450 million in debt and equity financings. John holds a Master of Public Administration from Columbia University and a Bachelor of Arts from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

  • Bruno Erckmam is the Head of Operations & Marketing at Cloud Hill Capital. Over six years he has built go-to-market strategy across ecommerce, fintech, financial services and professional services, developing a sharp instinct for how companies find and hold the attention of the people who matter most to them. His work sits where most firms have a gap: between the quality of what they do and the reach of who knows about it. Bruno closes that gap.

    Prior to Cloud Hill Capital, he led marketing and revenue operations at Fundraisr, an AI-powered operating system for investor relations teams, and held a senior role at Avalanche Capital, a capital advisory firm operating across private markets.
